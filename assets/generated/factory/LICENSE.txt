LAST LIGHT Procedural Asset Factory V1
Original geometry, materials, labels and rendered PNGs created for LAST LIGHT.
No external models, textures, fonts or proprietary game artwork are used in
rendered assets. Blender's built-in vector font renders original short labels.

The generated geometry descriptions and rendered sprite assets in this directory
are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
To the extent possible under law, the authors waive all copyright and related
rights to these original assets. Provided without warranty.

Source recipes: tools/asset_factory/{catalog.py,geometry.py,render_batch.py}
Calibration: tools/render_isometric.py
Source provenance and hashes: registry.json and manifest.json
Contact-sheet captions use system DejaVu Sans when available; this font is not
bundled. Contact sheets are documentation, not source textures.
