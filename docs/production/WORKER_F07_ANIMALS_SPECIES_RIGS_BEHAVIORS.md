## 06 — Species taxonomy and anatomy classes

Notion's candidate species list: cattle, chicken, pig, sheep, turkey, deer, mouse, rabbit, raccoon and rat; produce only actually A00-approved masters. Implement distinct anatomical templates for large hoofed quadrupeds, pigs/stocky quadrupeds, biped birds, rabbits/lagomorphs with powerful hind limbs, and small mammals/rodents with tails/paws. ## 12 — Foot planting, gait phases and animal IK

Require stable ground_centroid root pivot, credible stride length/speed, species appropriate front/hind leg coordination or alternating bird stance, and contact markers. No foot skating during support phases, no four hoof legs phase-locked into a cartoon bounce, no inverted joints or pelvis jump between camera views. Graze and drink motions must reach food/water without the muzzle intersecting terrain. Rabbit hopping uses coordinated hind push, not a generic cow walk. Produce loop contact metadata and per-foot socket positions for actual frames.
