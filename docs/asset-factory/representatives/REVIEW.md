# 20-object review gate

Blender 4.3.2 CPU Cycles, sixteen samples, fixed 80×40 projection. The first trial was generated and visually inspected before the 300-object run. Twenty names span every category: four food, three medical, three tools, three crafting, three household and four furniture.

Initial defects corrected: clipped hatchet icon (camera centering used an object-weighted mean rather than projection bounds); hammer head/handle proportions; stacked-ring crockery (replaced with continuous shared hollow shell); soft packaging contours and case latches. The full-batch validation subsequently found a floating/thin folded textile recipe; it was corrected and all sprites regenerated. Later review distinguished generic duplicate icons using actual packaging labels, material choices, non-uniform dimensions and workbench geometry.

Final re-render of the same 20 representatives: 9.91 seconds, 72 frames (20 ground, 20 inventory, 32 held for four objects). Validator PASS: RGBA, clipping, calibration anchors, scale, palette constraints and all files. PNG bytes: 301,884; decoded RGBA: 1,499,212. Twenty distinct inventory icon pixel hashes. See sample contact sheet, physical ground-scale comparison and held-direction sheet in this folder.

Inspection: coherent subdued materials and lighting, no proprietary artwork or cartoon outlines. Tin/bandage/tool ground sprites are naturally small at real scale; inventory icons intentionally fit their canvas. Geometry remains deliberately modular and simplified. Some specialized semantic types share a generic silhouette and need more bespoke detailing before final production approval. Held anchors are generic grip origins; no character rig or attack animation integration has been verified. Approval by the owner and actual iPhone review remain pending.
